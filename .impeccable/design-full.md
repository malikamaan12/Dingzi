# Design System: dingzi

## Overview
**Creative North Star: "The Volt Ringer"** — Bright guest menus & Midnight Indigo Order Prep.

## Colors
Primary `#6236FF`/`#4B1FE0`; Ready `#C8FF42` (`#17132E` ink); Neutral `#17132E`, `#241E42`, `#FFFFFF`, `#F6F5FC`. Zero gold/`#000000`.

## Typography
Plus Jakarta Sans, IBM Plex Sans Arabic (`RTL`), JetBrains Mono (`tabular-nums`). Lowercase `dingzi`.

## Shapes
`24%` squircles, `20px` cards, `28px` sheets, `9999px` pills.

## Components
`44px+` controls (`#4B1FE0` focus), `16px` inputs, `#C8FF42` banners.

## Do's and Don'ts
- **Do** keep café themes on menus with a `0.65` `Powered by dingzi` stamp.
- **Don't** capitalize `dingzi` or use gradient text, emoji icons, or `<44px` targets.
